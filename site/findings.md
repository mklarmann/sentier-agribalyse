## How to verify a finding

Open its GitHub discussion and check the same file in the same release. Reply with what you found, including a different result. The public record credits the person who identified the finding and the person who checked it. [How to post a verification](take-part.md#verify-a-finding).

## What the statuses mean

<!-- generated: status-vocabulary -->

A finding is resolved when a new AGRIBALYSE release or a dated correction to the affected file addresses it. The record links to that version. ADEME’s reply, a community proposal and a change in the local import each have their own place in the record. [Feedback to ADEME](feedback.md).
