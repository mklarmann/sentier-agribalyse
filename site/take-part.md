# Take part

Use this site to look up a food, check a release or report something you found. A browser and a public GitHub comment are enough for many checks. The LCI Workbench and the [local build](build.md) are for people investigating models or comparing inventories.

## Verify a finding

<!-- generated: second-measurement-list -->

1. Choose a [finding](findings.md) and open its GitHub discussion. Check the named release and file yourself.
2. Comment with your result. You can write **“I checked AGRIBALYSE [release], [file and product code or flow]. I found [same or different result]. I looked at [field, unit or steps].”**
3. A maintainer links your comment and credits your public handle in the record. A different result helps narrow down what happened. Maintainers update the status.

## Identify a finding

Start with one sentence describing what you noticed. Choose question, defect or difference. Include the release, product or process identifier, file or field, and enough detail for another person to check it. Share public source references. Keep licensed ecoinvent exchanges in your local build.

[Report a finding on GitHub](https://github.com/sentier-dev/sentier-agribalyse/issues/new?title=%5BFinding%5D+&body=%23%23%23+What+kind+of+finding+is+it%3F%0AQuestion%0A%0A%23%23%23+ADEME+release+it+was+observed+in%0A3.2%0A%0A%23%23%23+What+else+it+concerns%0A%5BFood+or+process+code%2C+flow+or+parameter%5D%0A%0A%23%23%23+What+the+data+says%0A%5BWhat+you+noticed%2C+with+units+where+relevant%5D%0A%0A%23%23%23+How+you+measured+it%0A%5BFile%2C+field+and+steps+another+person+can+follow%5D) · [Browse the existing GitHub discussions](https://github.com/sentier-dev/sentier-agribalyse/issues).

## Contribute a change

If you have a better mapping, a parameter scenario or an inventory model, describe what it changes and how someone can test it. The [contribution record](contributions.md) shows who did the work, the checks and the review decision. [Propose a contribution on GitHub](https://github.com/sentier-dev/sentier-agribalyse/issues/new?title=%5BContribution%5D%20&body=Proposed%20change%3A%20%0AAGRIBALYSE%20release%3A%20%0AEvidence%20and%20how%20to%20test%20it%3A%20).
