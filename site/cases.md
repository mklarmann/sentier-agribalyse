# Cases

A case follows one checkable observation about AGRIBALYSE or its import. Open a case to see who raised it, what evidence supports it and what happened next. Its number stays with it through review.

<!-- generated: case-register -->

To repeat an observation, open its [finding](findings.md) and follow the recorded method. [Proposed changes](contributions.md) lists candidate corrections, mappings and scenarios with their checks and review decisions. [Feedback to ADEME](feedback.md) records which findings reached the publisher, any reply and any correction in a published release.

To open a new case, [describe the observation and how to check it](take-part.md#identify-a-finding).

Under [Build](build.md), open **Comparison** and choose a case to see its evidence alongside the results for the whole build.

For corrections ADEME has already documented, including the packaging of seven foods, see [earlier reports and release notes](earlier-reports.md).
