AGRIBALYSE life-cycle inventory database

Feedback on ADEME's inventory data, from the people who use it

Users of the AGRIBALYSE life-cycle inventory database record here what they find in the data, check each other's measurements and report the findings to ADEME. The aim is a better database for everyone. ADEME decides what it changes.

Repeat a measurement
