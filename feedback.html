How to read this page

  • Reported to ADEME. A finding is shown as reported when its record holds the date of the report and a public link that shows it. Where a record holds no such date, the page says "not recorded".
  • ADEME's answer. An answer is shown as a short summary, with its date and a link to the place where it is recorded. The summary is attributed to ADEME as an agency. It names no person and quotes no message. An answer says what ADEME said. It does not show that the data has changed.
  • Resolved. A finding is resolved once an AGRIBALYSE release or file that the record names covers the point, whether or not that followed a report made here.
  • Repeated, not yet reported. A second person repeated the measurement and got the same result. The finding has not been reported to ADEME yet.

Where the evidence is

Each finding has its own page. The page says what was observed and where the finding is discussed. The findings page lists them all, and take part explains how to repeat a measurement.
